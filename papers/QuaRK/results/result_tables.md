### Hardware-aware settings

Simulated, not hardware-executed; no photonic parameter is trained, so no gradient is used. Common to every configuration: computation space FOCK, occupancy_readout=True; detector: ideal threshold detectors (click / no click), no dark counts, no loss; postselection: none; encoding: phases pi (1 - 1e-6) tanh(Pi x_t) on all modes at each step, between copies of one fixed random MZI mesh; measurement: click patterns, reduced to 1- and 2-mode click marginals. The simulator version is the one MerLin prints; the installed distribution is merlinquantum 0.4.2.

## Sweeps

No sweep selects a winner: each compares models or settings with its reference, under the paired 2 SE rule frozen before the runs. Inside every run, the Matérn ν and ξ, λ_reg and the tuned echo state network's setting (18 candidates) are chosen on validation data, the inner 20 % of the training windows; the solves are closed-form, so there are no checkpoints. Metric: test MSE on 2,000 held-out windows, lower is better; the tables show the normalized test MSE (test MSE / variance of the test labels), mean ± std over the seeds. Lowest: the candidate with the lowest mean per task; a tie means the next candidate is not separated from it.
